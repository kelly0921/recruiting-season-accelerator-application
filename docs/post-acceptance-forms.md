# Post-Acceptance Form Plan

The public application is Stage 1 and should remain a 7–10 minute selection form.
The forms below are private operational forms to send only after decisions are made.

## Founding Mentee Onboarding

Create a 15–20 minute form for the eight accepted mentees. Collect:

- Preferred name, optional pronouns, time zone, and participation accommodations
- Availability for all four group sessions and both individual sessions
- Detailed target roles and opportunity types
- Current opportunity-discovery and tracking process
- ApplyFirst baseline, account information, and opportunities already under consideration
- Detailed conference status, event names, dates, funding status, concerns, and testing availability
- The part of the participant's background that is hardest to position
- Questions for the resume review, workshop topics, and one possible group question
- Separate choices about hot seats, anonymous discussion, and sharing materials with the cohort
- Separate optional permissions for recordings, screenshots, quotes, testimonials, and public recognition
- Confirmation of privacy, conduct, feedback, pilot, and no-guarantee expectations

Every public-use permission must be separate and optional. Participation cannot depend
on agreeing to a testimonial or public identification.

## Extended Beta Onboarding

Create a shorter form for up to seven additional ApplyFirst testers. Collect only:

- Role and opportunity interests
- Current opportunity-discovery and tracking habits
- ApplyFirst baseline and account information
- Conference status and playbook-testing availability when relevant
- Commitment to short feedback prompts through December 15
- Separate privacy and optional permission preferences

Do not request a resume, individual-session questions, detailed workshop preferences,
or scheduling for benefits that beta-only participants do not receive.

## Information to Exclude

Do not request Social Security numbers, full birth dates, home addresses, immigration
documents or detailed immigration status, financial-account information, medical
information, school credentials, or GPA. Collect only the minimum information needed
to operate the program and evaluate the pilot.
